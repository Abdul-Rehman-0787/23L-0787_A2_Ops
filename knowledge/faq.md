# VisionGuard FAQ

## What file types and sizes can I upload?
You can upload JPG and PNG images up to 5 MB and up to 20 million pixels. Other file types are refused with a message.

## Are my images stored?
No. An image is analysed in memory and is not saved. Only these details are saved with your result: the cleaned file name, a SHA-256 hash of the file, its size, width and height, the verdict, the probability, the threshold, the model name and version, the processing time and the date.

## Who can see my history?
Only you. Each user sees only their own results, and Artist only sees your own results too.

## How do I log in?
You log in with your email and password, then type a 6-digit code that is emailed to you. The code is valid for 5 minutes, and you can ask for a new code once a minute.

## What happens after wrong passwords or codes?
After 5 wrong passwords, or 5 wrong codes, the account is locked for 15 minutes. During the lock even the correct password is refused.

## Why was my painting or illustration flagged as AI?
The model reacts to style more than to real signs of AI generation. Human-made paintings, illustrations and collages with bright colours or painterly texture are often flagged as AI. This is a known weakness of a model trained on a small dataset.

## Why was an AI portrait labelled Real?
Photorealistic AI portraits that look like ordinary stock photos are a known weakness. The model learned to expect colourful fantasy art from the AI class, so natural-looking AI photos can pass as Real.

## Can I use a verdict as proof?
No. A verdict is a model estimate that can be wrong in both directions. Do not use it as proof or as evidence in a dispute.

## Which model made my result?
The model name and version are saved in your History next to every result. The current model is visionguard version 2.

## Does Artist see my images?
No. Artist only reads text: your last few results (file name, verdict, probability), your recent chat messages and the reference documents. Images are never sent to Artist.
